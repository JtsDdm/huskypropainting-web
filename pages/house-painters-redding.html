From Faded Red
To Warm Greige

Decades of Northern California sun had left this home's dark red wood siding chalked and weathered, with bare board showing through on the south-facing walls.

We power washed the entire envelope, scraped and sanded the failing areas, caulked every joint and primed all bare wood before a single finish coat went on. Siding, trim, fascia and the carport were all repainted — taking the house from the darkest color on the road to a warm greige with crisp white trim.

  • Wood siding & trim
  • Carport included
  • Full prep & prime
  • Two finish coats

A Complete Color
Change On Stucco

Washed-out mauve and tired tan across a two-story stucco facade, with paint failing on the front steps and column bases — the architecture was there, but the color had given up.

We masked the full elevation, prepped and primed every failing surface, then repainted in a two-tone terracotta and sand scheme with brilliant white columns and deep red steps. Same house, same architecture, completely different presence from the street.

  • Two-story stucco
  • Full color change
  • Columns & steps
  • Masked & primed
